About the System

Smart Chennai Flood Risk Analytics combines spatial information and machine learning to evaluate flood conditions at ward level.

The analysis incorporates historical flood evidence, inundation points, hazard zones, drainage flow areas, waterbody coverage, river coverage and rainfall-related features.

Ward-level analysis is generated from the Greater Chennai Corporation ward boundaries and the underlying flood analysis grid.

Analysis framework: spatial feature engineering, ward-level aggregation and machine-learning-based flood-risk classification.
